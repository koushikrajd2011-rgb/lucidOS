lucidOS

Version 1.0.0 · Web Edition

lucidOS is a lightweight, fully web-based desktop operating system that runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no login, no limits — just open it and start exploring.

Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, lucidOS lets you open apps, drag windows, and experience a real desktop environment without ever leaving your browser tab.
